Rules and governance

International governing body

International Ski Mountaineering Federation (ISMF)

The ISMF is the world governing body for ski mountaineering and writes the sport rules used at races worldwide. Skimo Canada is Canada’s national federation and a member of the ISMF. The CMR Skimo Race is sanctioned by Skimo Canada and follows the ISMF sport rules.
