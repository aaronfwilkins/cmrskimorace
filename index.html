CMR Skimo Race

Ski mountaineering racing at Castle Mountain Resort, Alberta

January 8–10, 2027

Three days. Three races. One mountain.

The Castle Mountain Resort Skimo Race is a weekend of ski mountaineering racing in the Rockies of southwest Alberta. Skin up, boot-pack the steep parts, rip skins and ski down, then do it again. Race hard in the competitive field or take on the recreational course at your own pace.

Friday, January 8

Vertical

One climb, start to finish, from Whiskey Jack to the top of the T-Rex T-Bar.

449 m up

Saturday, January 9

Sprint

Short and fast: skinning, a boot-pack and a slalom descent. Qualifiers, heats and finals.

65 m laps

Sunday, January 10

Individual

The main event across Gravenstafel and Haig, including North Peak and the Chutes descent.

Up to 1,505 m
