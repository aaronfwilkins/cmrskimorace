Registration for the 2027 race is open.

Registration is open

Registration for the 2027 Castle Mountain Resort Skimo Race, January 8 to 10, is open on Zone4. Register online, or scan the QR code to go straight to the registration page.

Register on Zone4

Before you register

  • Choose your races. Vertical on Friday, January 8, Sprint on Saturday, January 9, Individual on Sunday, January 10. Enter one, two or all three.
  • Choose your division. Adult (18+) Competitive or Recreational, Junior (under 18), or Masters (35+).
  • Check the gear list. The Individual race requires full avalanche safety equipment. See Race Info for what each race needs.
  • On race weekend. Waivers and bib pickup are in the Day Lodge at Castle Mountain Resort.

Questions? Email info@cmrskimorace.ca.
