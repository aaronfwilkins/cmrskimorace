Sign-up for the 2027 race opens soon.

Registration is coming soon

The registration link for the 2027 Castle Mountain Resort Skimo Race will be posted here as soon as it opens. You can also scan the QR code to go to the registration page.

Before you register

  • Choose your races. Vertical on Friday, Sprint on Saturday, Individual on Sunday. Enter one, two or all three.
  • Choose your division. Adult (18+) Competitive or Recreational, Junior (under 18), or Masters (35+).
  • Check the gear list. The Individual race requires full avalanche safety equipment. See Race Info for what each race needs.
  • On race weekend. Waivers and bib pickup are in the Day Lodge at Castle Mountain Resort.

Questions in the meantime? Email info@cmrskimorace.ca.
